Hi Dan — the cutoff is 18 September, and it was set because of the upstream incident on the 19th.

Until then the rollup ran to the date it ran on #12. On the morning of 19 September the upstream orders table went from 1 284 102 rows to 61 #86, and the RCA that afternoon found that everything after 08:40 was inconsistent — duplicated and missing keys #r12 #90.

The call on the 21st settled on using nothing after the 18th, and that went on the record the same afternoon as a constraint on the order load #94 #95. Resuming from the 19th and patching the gap was raised on the 25th and dropped, because upstream had not been confirmed clean #101.

One thing worth saying out loud: it was meant to be temporary. The constraint still reads “for now”, and nothing on record says when it should be lifted — that is a gap, not a decision. Your own question about moving it to the 30th is on the record too #r14. INC-4471 is linked if you would rather read it yourself; it last opened on 11 October #r12.

provLedger

One project, seen three ways — and every piece of knowledge it holds carries the source it came from.

across nodesthe map and the data flow: what is read, what processes it, what comes out.
across timeone thing, everything that happened to it. Append-only — nothing is overwritten.
across taskswhat each run read, what it decided, what it rejected, and how it turned out.

A question nobody on the team can answer, the record answering it, and then how that record gets made. The ledger in this walkthrough is a worked example, not a live database.
